A personal studio

Make the video,
read the numbers,
publish it.

Thousand Turns is a local-first video studio for one YouTube channel. It researches a topic, writes and storyboards it, animates every frame in HTML, narrates it, checks the render, reads the channel's own analytics — and, when asked, uploads the finished file.

Runs on your machine

The studio is a command-line tool. Projects, renders, voice files and analytics snapshots stay on your computer. There is no account, no cloud workspace and no server of ours.

Connects only to the platforms

With your permission it reads your YouTube and Instagram statistics through their official APIs and, on an explicit command, uploads a finished video. Nothing is shared with anyone else.

You decide every release

Uploads happen only when you run the publish command yourself. The default visibility is private; public and unlisted have to be asked for. The studio never posts on its own.

What it does with your data

Three things, and nothing else:

  • Reads your channel's statistics — views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, audience, comments — to show you what worked and what to change in the next video.
  • Writes a local analytics report — a single HTML page and a few data files in the project folder on your computer.
  • Uploads a video when you tell it to — with the title, description and tags you approved, at the privacy you chose.

Credentials are stored only on your machine, in a private folder readable by your user account. You can revoke access at any time from your Google or Meta account settings. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Why this page exists

Google requires an app that uses its APIs to have a public home page and a privacy policy. This is that home page. The app is personal software, not a service: no sign-up, no newsletter, no tracking, no cookies.

Questions, corrections or a request to delete anything: open an issue at github.com/aayushkedawat/thousand.turns.
